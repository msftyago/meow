## The Beginning

Holy Fawn is my most beloved band ever. Ryan Osterman is THE guy, i relate to him in some ways i can not describe, it's all so relatable,  but it feels like i knew them since forever. 

It was the summer of 2024, July 11. Was thundering, raining strong. And I was in my room, taking pictures of thunder bolts. Then [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=THLYcmmnoL0) song came in on my recommendation. It was matching the vibe, like we say in Uzbekistan "Like a stone set in a ring".
